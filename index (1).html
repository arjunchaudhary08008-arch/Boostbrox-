Guides

Beginner tips that work in most Farming Simulator games, including FS25, FS23 and FS20.

How to earn money fast
  • Take missions or contracts early, if your game has them. They pay without buying expensive machines.
  • Start with cheap crops and a basic tractor. Buy bigger machines only after your income is steady.
  • Prices can change. When you can, store the crop and sell it when the price is higher.
  • Keep some cash for fuel, seeds and repairs.
Crop cycle: from soil to sale
  1. Prepare the soil with a plow or cultivator.
  2. Sow the seeds with a seeder.
  3. Add fertilizer if your game has it, for a better harvest.
  4. Wait until the crop is ripe, then harvest it.
  5. Sell the crop at a selling point, or store it for later.
Beginner mistakes to avoid
  • Buying a big, expensive machine too early.
  • Harvesting before the crop is ripe.
  • Running out of fuel far from the farm.
  • Starting too many fields at once and having no money left for the next step.
Tractor and tool tips
  • Match the tool to the tractor. Heavy tools need a stronger tractor.
  • Drive slowly when plowing, or the tractor may struggle.
  • Park near your next field to save time and fuel.
